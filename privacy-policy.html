Dot Island: Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 2026-11-05

1. Who is responsible

Teymur Quliyev ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: teymurquliyev36@gmail.com.

Dot Island is an independent app. It is not an official Nothing app and is not affiliated with Nothing Technology Limited.

2. The short version

• The content of your notifications, calls, media and screen is handled only on your device. We do not receive it and we do not run servers that collect it.

• The only data that leaves your device is what Google collects to show ads (in the settings screens only) and to process purchases.

3. What the App handles on your device

• Notifications from other apps: sender, title, text, icons, buttons. Used to show them on the island and to keep them in the in-app tray. The tray is stored as text in the App's private storage on your phone, up to 200 items, and you can switch it off or clear it at any time. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

• Media information (track, artist, artwork, playback position) so the island can show and control playback.

• Which app is in front, to hide the island where you asked.

• Name and battery level of connected headphones (only if you allow the Bluetooth permission).

• The names of installed apps, shown in the settings so that you can choose apps.

• Your settings and purchase status, stored on your device.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the App.

4. What third parties receive

• Google AdMob (advertising, free version only, settings screens only). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Where the law requires it, ad personalisation depends on your consent choices.

• Google Play (purchases). Google processes your payment and tells the App whether you own a purchase. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Why we process data and on what basis

To provide the features you ask for (performance of the agreement and your consent when you switch on the permissions), to show advertising that keeps the free version available (your consent or our legitimate interest, depending on where you live), and to meet legal duties.

6. How long

Data on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. We hold no personal data on our servers. Third parties keep data under their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent, to object, to restrict processing, to data portability and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold your notification data, you exercise most of these by changing or deleting data on your own device. For anything else write to teymurquliyev36@gmail.com. Google handles requests about its own processing.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

Data stays in the App's private storage on your device and is protected by Android. No method of storage is perfectly secure; keep your phone locked and updated.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is important, the App will ask you to accept the new version.
